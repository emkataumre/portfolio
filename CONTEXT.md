# Portfolio

Emil Vladinov's personal portfolio site. It shows how Emil works with coding agents to ship real software, and it is itself built that way.

## Language

**Reader**:
The person the site is written for: an engineering lead or senior peer who judges whether Emil ships with agents. A recruiter must get the gist from the top fold.
_Avoid_: Visitor, user, audience

**Cursor Avatar**:
The hero element. A real headshot that swaps between nine pre-shot Poses so the face looks toward the cursor.
_Avoid_: Following avatar, 3D avatar, tracking photo

**Pose**:
One of the nine head-direction photos of the Cursor Avatar: Center, Left, Right, Up, Down, and the four diagonals.
_Avoid_: Frame, sprite, direction image

**Working Method**:
The section that describes how Emil plans, drives, reviews, and tests agent-produced code. It stands in for private work that cannot be shown.
_Avoid_: Methodology, process page, case studies

**Testimony**:
A claim about private work that the Reader cannot verify by link, such as "shipped to production, passed senior review". The site states Testimony plainly and never dresses it up as proof.
_Avoid_: Proof, evidence

**Build Log**:
The public record of how this site was built with agents. The one artifact the Reader can verify.
_Avoid_: Changelog, devlog, blog
